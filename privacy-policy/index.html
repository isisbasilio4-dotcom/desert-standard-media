Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026 · Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how Desert Standard Media (“we,” “us,” or “our”), a marketing agency based in the Coachella Valley, California, handles information collected through desertstandardmedia.com, its landing pages and application forms, our advertising lead forms, and related business communications.

The essentials: We use the information you provide to review your business goals, assess whether our services fit, and respond to your enquiry. Website forms are processed through Formspree. Applying does not subscribe you to marketing emails or texts.

Please do not submit patient records, health information, payment-card details, passwords, or other sensitive personal information. Our forms are for business enquiries, not medical care.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details: your name, email address, optional phone number, preferred contact method, and information in your messages.
  • Business details: your med spa or business name, role, website or Instagram handle, and city and state.
  • Application details: services you want to promote, growth goals, marketing challenges, paid-advertising history, current and planned advertising spend, readiness to invest in agency fees, desired start date, and any additional details you choose to provide.
  • Campaign details: on the ad landing page, campaign tags in the page URL, such as source, medium, campaign, content, and term, may be included with your application. We also include a submission reference and the page source to identify the enquiry.
  • Technical information: hosting and form-service providers may process your IP address, browser or device information, referring website, and request or submission time to deliver and protect their services.

When you submit an Instagram or Facebook lead form, we receive the fields you choose to submit through that form. Meta also processes information under its own privacy policy. If you contact us directly, we receive the information you include in that communication.

Required fields are marked in our forms. You can leave optional fields blank. Without the details needed to review or respond to an enquiry, we may be unable to proceed with it.

2. How we use your information

  • Review applications and understand your business goals and service needs.
  • Assess service fit and contact you about a discovery call or other next steps.
  • Respond to questions, prepare relevant service proposals, and maintain business communications.
  • Understand which campaigns generate enquiries and improve our forms and marketing.
  • Prevent spam and misuse, troubleshoot delivery, maintain records, and meet applicable legal obligations.

We may respond by email or, when you provide a phone number and request a call, by phone. Submitting an application does not enroll you in a marketing-email list or authorize automated marketing texts.

3. Service providers and disclosures

We use providers to operate the website and handle enquiries. Information may be available to people helping us respond to your enquiry only as needed for that work.

  • Formspree: receives, processes, and stores website-form submissions and sends notification emails to us. It may also process technical information for delivery, security, and spam prevention. See Formspree’s Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts: the main website and ad landing page load fonts from Google. Your browser makes requests to Google’s servers, which can disclose technical request information such as your IP address. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Meta and communication providers: Instagram or Facebook processes interactions with our ads, lead forms, and profiles. Our email or other communication providers also process messages used to respond to enquiries. See Meta’s Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law or legal process, protect rights and safety, prevent fraud, or resolve disputes. We do not sell application information or provide it to other businesses for their own cross-context behavioral advertising.

Service providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate. Their own privacy terms explain their separate processing practices.

4. Cookies, campaign tags, and tracking choices

The current website does not install its own advertising cookies, Meta Pixel, or third-party analytics tags. The application keeps unfinished answers in memory while the page is open; it does not write those answers to browser local storage. Refreshing or closing the page clears those unfinished answers.

Campaign tags identify the ad or campaign associated with an application. They are read from the landing-page URL and sent with the form; they are not, by themselves, cross-site tracking cookies.

The site does not currently change its behavior in response to a browser “Do Not Track” signal. It does not currently use website tracking to follow visitors across unrelated sites. Third parties such as Meta, Google, GitHub, and Formspree may independently collect technical or interaction information as described in their policies, and may handle browser privacy signals under their own practices.

You can use your browser’s cookie and privacy controls and the privacy settings offered by those providers. If we add analytics or advertising tracking, we will update this policy and provide any notices or choices required for that change.

5. Retention and security

We retain enquiry and application information for as long as reasonably needed to respond, evaluate service fit, maintain a business relationship or necessary records, and meet legal or dispute-resolution obligations. Retention depends on the purpose, the status of our conversation, and applicable requirements.

Formspree’s archive period depends on the account plan and settings. Copies delivered to our inbox are separate records and are not automatically removed just because a provider’s archive period ends.

The website and form endpoint use HTTPS. We use reasonable safeguards for the information we handle, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

You may ask to access, correct, or delete information you submitted, ask a privacy question, or request that we stop further follow-up. Contact us using the method below. We may need to verify your identity or association with the enquiry before acting on a request. Please do not send passwords, identity documents, or patient information.

We will consider requests in accordance with applicable law. Some information may need to be retained for legal obligations, security, or an existing business relationship. Your legal rights may vary depending on where you live and which laws apply.

7. Business audience and external links

Our services and application forms are intended for adult business owners and representatives, not children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can review it.

Links to Instagram, Facebook, and other external websites lead to services with their own privacy practices. This policy does not control those services.

8. Changes to this policy

We will post updates on this page and revise the “Last updated” date. For material changes, we will also provide an appropriate notice on the website or through another suitable method when required.

9. Contact Desert Standard Media

For privacy questions or requests, send a private message to @desertstandardmedia on Instagram. Start your message with “Privacy request” and describe what you would like us to review. Include enough information to identify your enquiry, but avoid sensitive information.
