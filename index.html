04 / A little clarity

Before we
get started.

Who is this for?

Med spas looking for a more intentional way to attract enquiries and book consultations. We start by discussing your services, market, team capacity, and goals to assess fit.

Do you handle the ads and appointment setting?

Our offer combines paid advertising, lead generation, and appointment-setting support. The channels, follow-up responsibilities, and booking handoff are agreed before work begins.

Is advertising spend included?

Advertising spend and agency fees are separate. We discuss a suitable budget and scope after understanding your goals, market, and appointment capacity.

Can you guarantee bookings or revenue?

No specific number of bookings or revenue is guaranteed. Results depend on your market, offer, budget, follow-up, and other factors. We focus on transparent measurement and ongoing improvement.

How do we begin?

Start a conversation on Instagram. Share your med spa name, location, and the service you want to grow. We’ll discuss whether there’s a fit and the next step for a consultation.
