Before you apply

A few things
worth knowing.

What happens after I apply?

We review your business and goals, then contact you about next steps for a free discovery call. Applying does not automatically book a time.

What does the discovery call cost?

The discovery call is free. If we decide to work together, the service scope, agency fees, and advertising budget are discussed separately.

Do I need to be running ads already?

No. The application is suitable whether you’re new to paid ads, have tried them before, or want to improve current advertising.

Can you guarantee bookings?

No specific number of leads, bookings, or revenue is guaranteed. Results depend on your market, offer, budget, follow-up, and other factors.
