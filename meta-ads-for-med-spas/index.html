Before your first campaign.

Can we work together if my med spa has never run ads?

Yes. This service is designed for med spa owners preparing their first Instagram and Facebook campaign. We review your treatment, appointment capacity, budget, and follow-up process to assess fit.

What are Meta ads?

Meta ads are paid placements on platforms including Instagram and Facebook. For a med spa, a campaign can introduce a treatment to prospective patients in its service area and invite them to inquire.

How much should I budget?

Your agency fee and the money spent on advertising are separate costs. We discuss your goals, local market, and campaign scope before proposing a budget and clearly identify both costs.

Who responds to the inquiries?

Your practice handles inquiry follow-up and scheduling unless a different arrangement is expressly included in your service agreement. We agree on the handoff before the campaign launches.

How will we evaluate the campaign?

We review ad spend, inquiry volume, and cost per inquiry. When your practice provides booking and sales records, we can also assess cost per booked consultation, attended appointment, and paying patient.

Are appointments or revenue guaranteed?

Campaign outcomes vary with the treatment, offer, local competition, budget, and your practice’s response and consultation process. We agree on measurable goals and review actual performance with you.
